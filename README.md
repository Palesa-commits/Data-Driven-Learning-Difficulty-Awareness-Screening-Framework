A data-driven screening framework designed to help parents, teachers and schools identify early warning signs of potential learning difficulties and support early educational intervention.

# Project Overview
Many learners with possible learning difficulties are identified late or remain undiagnosed and therefore unsupported, particularly in under-resourced communities. This project explores how academic performance patterns and related risk factors can be used to support early screening for potential learning difficulties.

The project combines data analysis, risk scoring, a structured screening framework and printable screening questionnaires designed to encourage early identification, awareness and educational intervention.

 # Methodology
### Data Analysis
Exploratory and statistical analysis was conducted on a student academic performance dataset from Kaggle, focusing on reading, writing and mathematics scores. Percentile analysis, score distributions, averages and risk-level comparisons were used identify performance trends and potential academic difficulty patterns.

### Academic Risk Scoring
A simplified academic risk scoring framework was developed using subject-level performance thresholds. Students were categorized into:

Low Risk

Moderate Risk

High Risk

### Contextual Analysis
Additional factors such as lunch status, test preparation participation and parental education levels were analyzed to identify broader educational risk patterns.

### Screening Questionnaire Development

Age-appropriate printable screening questionnaires were developed for:

Ages 6–9

Ages 10–12

Ages 13–15

### Synthetic Dataset Testing
A synthetic dataset was created to test and demonstrate the screening framework in practice.
